
	CONCEPTS: WEB DESIGN

	-- COLOR
		-- Be aware of the mood of your color palette
			RED:  love, energy, intensity
			YELLOW: joy, intellect, attention
			GREEN: freshness, safety, growth
			BLUE: stability, trust, serenity
			PURPLE: royalty, wealth, femininity


	-- TYPOGRAPHY
		-- 2 large font families:
			--  Serif: which have little feet at the end of their central beams; inspired by the marble carvings of days of yore
				-- Mode it conveys: Traditional, Stable, Respectable

			--  San-Serif: which have perfect right angles
				-- Mode is conveys: Sensible, Simple, Straightforward

		-- Stick to 2 fonts in your design; keep the same mode


	-- User Interface Design
		-- 1) Hierarchy: Establish hierarchy with the use of "color and size of text/objects" to convey the important elements first
			-- Use color contrasting techniques to establish hierarchy
			-- Use size - bigger items draw you to it

		-- 2) Layout
		--	characters per line of text: 40 - 60 characters

	-- User Experience Design
		-- Is supposed to be un-intrusive
		-- Is supposed to be invisible
		-- Key factors:
			1)	Keep things simple
			2)	Be consistent
			3)	Be aware of Reading Patters
				-- F Patten: most common, eye starts at top left corner and then tracks the right and then it flips back again browsing down the left gutter
				-- Z Pattern: left to right then zigzagging down all the way
