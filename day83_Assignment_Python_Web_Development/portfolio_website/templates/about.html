ABOUT ME

My Story

I'm Katrina Smith, a software engineer and UI/UX professional with a passion for transforming complex problems into practical, intuitive digital experiences.

My career has taken me across software development, business analysis, UX design, enterprise architecture, operations, and information security. That range gives me a valuable perspective: great software isn't just about writing good code. It requires understanding the people using it, the business problem behind it, and the systems that have to support it.

Today, much of my work sits at the intersection of software engineering and user experience. I design and develop enterprise interfaces, establish reusable UI patterns, translate Figma concepts into scalable applications, modernize legacy systems, and collaborate across product, design, engineering, network, and operations teams.
